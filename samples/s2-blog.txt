If you have ever struggled to stay consistent with a morning routine, you are not alone. Many people start the new year with ambitious plans, only to abandon them within a few weeks. The key is not willpower, but design. Start small by choosing one habit that takes less than five minutes, such as drinking a glass of water. Once that habit feels automatic, gradually add another. Most importantly, focus on progress rather than perfection. Over time, these small, intentional steps can build a routine that genuinely supports your energy and focus.

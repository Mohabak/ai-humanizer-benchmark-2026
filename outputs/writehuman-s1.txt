Remote work has changed how organizations handle productivity and wellness. Flexible work arrangements improve commute-related stress and increase job satisfaction. While these are work perks, not everyone enjoys the same benefits. Caregivers have a hard time maintaining focus and early career employees don’t have access to mentorships because of a lack of formal programs. Because of this, many companies have adopted a hybrid work model that focuses on having the flexibility to work from home and the structure to work from the office. In the end, how well companies address hybrid work models with clear guidelines and focused communication will dictate how successful they are.

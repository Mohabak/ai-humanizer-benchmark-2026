If you've ever found it hard to stick to a daily routine, you're in good company. A lot of us begin a New Year's resolution with big goals, however most fall off after only a couple of weeks. What matters isn't will power, it is how well something is designed. Take an easy first step by picking a simple (less than five minute) habit, like having a glass of water. After this has become a part of who you are, then continue with adding habits. It's essential to take a progressive approach rather than trying for perfect results. As you build routines with the purpose of energizing and focusing yourself, each of those little bits can create a solid foundation.

Remote work has changed the way companies think about getting things done and taking care of their employees. Studies show that letting people work from anywhere can make them happier with their jobs and less stressed from commuting. But not everyone gets the same benefits. People who take care of others at home often find it harder to focus, and new employees might miss out on learning from others in the office. So, a lot of companies are trying a mix of both - letting people work from home, but also having them come into the office sometimes. This way, people can still work independently, but also collaborate with their team. For this to work in the long run, companies need to have clear rules and make sure everyone is on the same page.

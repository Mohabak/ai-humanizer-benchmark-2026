Sticking to a morning routine can be tough - you're definitely not the only one who struggles with it. Lots of people start the year with big plans, but they often fizzle out after a few weeks. It's not about being strong-willed, though - it's really about setting things up to work for you. So, start with something tiny, like drinking a glass of water as soon as you wake up. It takes less than five minutes, and that's the point - it's easy to get into the habit. Once that feels like second nature, you can add something else in. And here's the thing: don't worry if it's not perfect. Just focus on making progress, one small step at a time. With a little patience, these tiny habits can add up to a routine that really works for you, giving you more energy and helping you stay focused.

If you’ve ever had trouble sticking to a morning routine, you’re not alone. Many people begin the new year with ambitious plans, only to abandon them after a few weeks. The key is design, not will power. Start small. Choose a habit that takes less than five minutes, like drinking a glass of water. When that feels natural, slowly add another one. Most importantly, concentrate on progress, not perfection. Over time, these small, deliberate actions can create a habit that really helps your energy and focus.

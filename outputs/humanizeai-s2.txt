If you’ve ever found it difficult to be consistent in your morning routine, there’s no need to worry, because many people find themselves in this situation. They start off the year with great intentions, only to give up after a couple of weeks. It’s all about the design; the first step should be small, like taking one action that takes no more than five minutes – for example, having a drink of water. When this one becomes routine, then you can take the next step. Above all else, remember to focus on progress, not perfection.

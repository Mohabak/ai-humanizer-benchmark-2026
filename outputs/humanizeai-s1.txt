Working from home has completely changed the way in which companies think about productivity and employee well-being. The literature suggests that working flexibly may help in increasing job satisfaction levels and decrease stress related to commuting. Nevertheless, these positive aspects do not equally apply to all workers. Workers who are responsible for caring for others find it more difficult to concentrate, and younger workers are deprived of informal mentoring that usually takes place at work. Therefore, many companies are starting to implement a hybrid system that is intended to unite the benefits of both options.
